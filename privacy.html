PRIVACY POLICY

GradeLab respects student privacy.

Effective date: October 19, 2026

What GradeLab accesses

GradeLab reads course names, released assignments, due dates, submission status, earned scores, and possible points from pages on https://www.gradescope.com/* while the user is signed in.

How data is used

This information is used only to render the user’s grade dashboard, calculate category averages, apply verified syllabus weights, and run temporary what-if calculations.

What GradeLab does not collect

GradeLab does not request or store passwords, copy or export authentication cookies, sell personal data, use grade information for advertising, or transmit grade information to the developer.

Local storage

What-if assignments stay in the extension. Optional grade-history snapshots are disabled by default and, when enabled, are stored only in Chrome’s local extension storage on the user’s device. Update requests created in the prototype are also local unless the user copies and sends them.

Third parties

Gradescope is operated independently and its own privacy terms apply. GradeLab is not affiliated with or endorsed by Gradescope, Turnitin, Carnegie Mellon University, or Google.

Deletion

Users can remove locally stored GradeLab data by disabling grade history, clearing the extension’s site data, or uninstalling the extension.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the GradeLab support page.
