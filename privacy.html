PRIVACY POLICY

GradeLab respects student privacy.

Effective date: October 19, 2026

What GradeLab accesses

GradeLab reads course names, assignment groups, category weights, released assignments, due dates, submission status, earned scores, and possible points from https://canvas.cmu.edu/*. For 15-112, it can read the student’s official grade breakdown from https://www.15-112.dev/*. When available, it supplements official sources with assignment details from https://www.gradescope.com/*. Access occurs only while the user is signed in.

How data is used

This information is used only to render the user’s grade dashboard, preserve Canvas category weights and posted totals, combine supplemental Gradescope scores, calculate category averages, and run temporary what-if calculations.

What GradeLab does not collect

GradeLab does not request or store passwords, copy or export authentication cookies, sell personal data, use grade information for advertising, or transmit grade information to the developer.

Advertising

GradeLab does not currently display ads or load an advertising network. The public website contains disabled ad placements for a possible future launch. Ads will not be enabled until the privacy notice, consent controls, and provider disclosures are updated. Private grade data will never be used for ad targeting.

Local storage

What-if assignments stay in the extension. Optional grade-history snapshots are disabled by default and, when enabled, are stored only in Chrome’s local extension storage on the user’s device. Update requests created in the prototype are also local unless the user copies and sends them.

Third parties

Canvas and Gradescope are operated independently and their own privacy terms apply. GradeLab is not affiliated with or endorsed by Instructure, Gradescope, Turnitin, Carnegie Mellon University, or Google.

Deletion

Users can remove locally stored GradeLab data by disabling grade history, clearing the extension’s site data, or uninstalling the extension.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the GradeLab support page.
